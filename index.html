Accelerating SDGs at AZMIU

The Azerbaijan University of Architecture and Construction drives innovation across the Sustainable Development Goals. Our multidisciplinary teams translate global insights into local solutions for resilient cities, clean water, inclusive education, and climate-ready infrastructure.

Flagship Research Initiatives

  • Green Campus Living Lab: Tracking energy efficiency retrofits and student-led audits that reimagine our campus as a climate-positive demonstrator.
  • Urban Resilience Design Studio: Co-developing adaptive planning models that support SDG 11 across Azerbaijan's urban and regional landscapes.
  • Water-Sensitive Building Materials: Advancing eco-conscious construction materials that protect water resources while elevating architectural excellence.
